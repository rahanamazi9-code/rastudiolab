Interior Renovation

Niha's Bedroom

Location

Shiraz, Iran

Year

2023

Scope

Interior Design

Designed for a young girl, this bedroom balances playfulness with a calm, timeless aesthetic. Natural wood, soft beige cabinetry, and a whimsical cartoon wallpaper create a warm environment that encourages both imagination and everyday living. A custom window bench forms a comfortable reading nook, while an integrated desk provides a dedicated space for learning and creativity. Beyond the visible elements, the design carefully resolves existing architectural constraints by concealing the wall-mounted air conditioning unit behind bespoke louvred cabinetry, integrating projecting structural columns into the joinery, and reworking the ceiling to create a cleaner and more cohesive spatial composition. The result is a functional, adaptable room where every element contributes to a unified and child-friendly environment.
